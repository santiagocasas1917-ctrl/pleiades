# pleiades
Problem: Students young women and career changers who want to get into tech often don't know anyone in the industry. Mentors can only take a few people at a time, and it's hard for a nonprofit to keep members coming back or to show that its programs actually help them grow.:how are we going to solve this problem? here is how: 1 on 1 mentorship burns out volunteers fast, and open-ended Slack groups usually go quiet.
Instead, put 6–8 folks on a similar path into a small pod with a mentor dropping in biweekly. Finishing a project or case study earns a warm intro rather than cold messaging strangers. Keep people showing up with short 8-week sprints building a project or prepping for interviews and bring recent job landers back as near-peer mentors. Skip huge surveys ,a quick 3 question check in at the start, midpoint, and six months out gives you real proof for donors without extra paperwork.
